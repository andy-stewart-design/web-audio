# Step 2.1 stateful noise DSP evidence

- Date: 2026-10-19
- Scope: worklet-local white/pink noise generation, deterministic timing, and statistical characterization. No adapter, engine registration, public API, or native browser integration is included.

## Implementation

- `packages/worklets/src/processors/noise-processor.ts` uses a nonzero-state xorshift32 PRNG. Seeds are internal numeric options; invalid or zero seeds resolve to a deterministic fallback. The sample loop allocates nothing and carries PRNG/filter state across process blocks.
- White output is uniform `[-1, 1)`, with no per-note normalization. Pink output uses the stateful seven-coefficient Paul Kellett-style filter, including the reference `-0.7616 * b5` feedback sign, and a fixed `0.33` output calibration selected to keep pink and white RMS levels approximately matched. A DC-blocking pole is scaled from `0.9999` at 48 kHz by `0.9999 ** (48000 / sampleRate)`; this is the sample-rate-aware part of the treatment and keeps finite-run mean bias below the stated gate.
- Start and stop frames gate exact sample boundaries. Constructor frame options support offline scheduling; `start`/`stop` control messages support cancellation and late timing updates. Completion posts `{ type: "ended" }` once and returns `false`; malformed options/messages are ignored without throwing.
- The Kellett spectral coefficients remain a fixed-rate approximation rather than being recomputed per sample rate. Cross-rate measurements at 44.1, 48, and 96 kHz validate that approximation over the required 100 Hz–8 kHz analysis band.
- Pink coefficient provenance: Paul Kellett, “Pink noise filter,” music-dsp.org, including the linked `pink.txt`: <https://www.musicdsp.org/en/latest/Filters/76-pink-noise-filter.html>. The implementation independently transcribes the published equations rather than copying source code; no third-party package or source file was added. The page identifies the author/source but does not publish a separate software license.

## Measurements and tests

- The focused suite uses 10-second runs, discards the first 2 seconds, and applies Hann-windowed Goertzel estimates averaged across five nearby frequencies at each octave from 125 Hz through 8 kHz. It checks three fixed seeds at 44.1, 48, and 96 kHz. Every run enforces `abs(mean) / RMS < 0.05`; spectral slopes and RMS gates use the mean of the three seed measurements.
- Representative 48 kHz, seed `0x12345678` results after settling: white mean approximately `-0.0018`, RMS `0.5777`, mean/RMS `0.0032`, PSD slope `+0.05 dB/octave`; pink mean approximately `-0.0076`, RMS `0.5705`, mean/RMS `0.0133`, PSD slope `-2.95 dB/octave`.
- Across the three-seed 48 kHz measurements, average pink RMS is approximately `0.5719`, average white RMS approximately `0.5773`, and average pink slope approximately `-3.17 dB/octave`.
