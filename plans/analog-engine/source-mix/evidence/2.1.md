- Date: 2026-10-26

## Implementation and review findings

The focused lifecycle review found one bounded runtime gap: `SupersawSource` handled its normal ended message but did not complete its shared voice when the unison worklet emitted `processorerror` or `error`. With a surviving sub, that could leave the retiring instrument waiting indefinitely. The source now treats those worklet notifications as completion, reports one contextual `console.error("Supersaw AudioWorklet processor failed", event)` with the original event payload, and removes the listeners during teardown. Its disconnect cleanup uses `try/finally`, so node disconnection is attempted even if port/listener cleanup throws. No defect was found in the existing shared ownership path; `Instrument` already disconnects completed siblings individually and releases shared nodes/bindings only after the whole composed voice is finished.

The existing fakes and assertions were extended rather than adding a new framework. Coverage now includes all six main/sub/noise completion orders, stale notifications, future cancellation beside an active composed voice, repeated destruction of all three layers, sub construction/setup/start failures after main allocation, unison processor failure with a surviving sub, two-voice detune LFO fan-out and per-voice cleanup, two-voice detune MIDI updates/subscription cleanup, per-voice LFO and MIDI sub-gain binding cleanup, graph replacement/original routing, invalid sub-schema preservation, and main-zero chord MIDI/duck behavior.
